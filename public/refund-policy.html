Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

Room & Apartment Bookings

When you cancelRefund
More than 48 hours before check-inFull deposit refunded
Within 48 hours of check-inDeposit is non-refundable
No-show (guest never arrives, no cancellation)Deposit is forfeited

To cancel a reservation, contact us at reservations@aureliahotel.com or call the front desk, quoting your reservation code (found on your confirmation, or via Track My Booking).

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the same MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money account the original payment came from. Processing typically takes 3–7 business days, depending on the provider.

Food Delivery Orders

Because food is prepared fresh to order, delivery orders cannot be cancelled once the kitchen has started preparing them. If your order arrives incorrect, incomplete, or you were charged in error, contact us within 24 hours at reservations@aureliahotel.com with your order number and we'll make it right — a replacement or a refund, at our discretion, depending on the situation.

Early Departure

Guests who choose to check out earlier than their booked dates are not entitled to a refund for the unused nights, unless agreed otherwise with management in advance.

Disputed or Failed Payments

If a Mobile Money payment is deducted from your account but your booking or order does not show as confirmed, contact us immediately with your payment reference — we will investigate with the payment provider and refund any payment that was taken without a corresponding confirmed booking.
