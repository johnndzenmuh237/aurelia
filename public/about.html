Our Story

An independent hotel, run like a modern business

Aurelia Hotel opened with one goal: give every guest a stay that feels effortless, from the first search on our website to the moment housekeeping resets the room after checkout. Behind the scenes, one connected system links reservations, the front desk, housekeeping, the restaurant and finance — so nothing gets double-entered and nothing falls through the cracks.

What guests notice

Accurate bills. Rooms that are actually ready when promised. A front desk that already knows who's arriving today. It's not flashy — it's just how a hotel should run.
