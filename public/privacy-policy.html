Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Aurelia Hotel ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and booking system. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it — in plain language.

1. Who we are

Aurelia Hotel, Rue de la Paix, Douala, Cameroon. Contact: reservations@aureliahotel.com, +237 6 00 00 00 00.

2. What we collect

We only collect what's needed to run your booking, order, or stay:

  • Booking a room or apartment: full name, phone number, email (optional), check-in/check-out dates, number of guests, special requests.
  • Ordering food delivery: full name, phone, WhatsApp number (optional), email (optional), delivery address, order notes.
  • Payment: the mobile money number you provide to MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money to authorize a charge. We never see or store your PIN, and we do not store card numbers because we don't accept cards — payment happens through the provider's own secure request-to-pay system.
  • Job applications: name, contact details, position applied for, and the experience/qualifications you choose to share.
  • Staff use of the internal system: a role and a name typed in at login (see our internal Security documentation for how staff sign in) — this is operational data, not marketing data, and is never shared externally.

We do not require you to create an account to book a room or order food. We do not ask for information we don't need — no ID documents, no date of birth, no payment card numbers.

3. How we use it

  • To create and manage your reservation or food order.
  • To process payment through MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money.
  • To contact you about your booking (confirmations, check-in reminders).
  • To respond to job applications or contact-form messages.
  • To operate the hotel internally — housekeeping, front desk, billing.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or third-party marketing.

4. Where it's stored

Booking, order, and operational data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database. Access is restricted to our own server, which enforces role-based access for staff — see our Security page for detail. We do not use browser tracking or analytics cookies (see our Cookies Policy).

5. How long we keep it

Reservation and payment records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and legal record-keeping, and may be deleted by hotel management when no longer required. You can ask us to delete your information by contacting us — see Section 8.

6. Who we share it with

  • MTN Mobile Money / Orange Money — to process your payment, only the amount, your mobile money number, and a reference are shared.
  • Google Firebase — our database and hosting infrastructure provider, bound by their own data-processing terms.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to: tell you what information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, or delete your information (subject to legal/accounting retention requirements). Contact us using the details in Section 1.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
